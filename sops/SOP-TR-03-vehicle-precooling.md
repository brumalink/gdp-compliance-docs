# SOP-TR-03 Vehicle pre-cooling

| Document ID | Version | Effective date | Owner | Approved by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SOP-TR-03 | 1.0 | 2025-03-26 | Piotr Kaczmarek | Joanna Kowalczyk |

## 1. Purpose

Make sure the cargo space has reached the product temperature range **before** loading.

## 2. Procedure

1. Start the refrigeration unit at least **30 minutes** before loading (60 minutes when outside temperature is above 25 °C).
2. Check the display and the BL-T3 tracker reading in the driver app. Both must be within the product profile.
3. Record the pre-cooling check in the driver app (photo of the display is required).
4. If the range is not reached within 60 minutes, do not load – inform the dispatcher and open a deviation.
