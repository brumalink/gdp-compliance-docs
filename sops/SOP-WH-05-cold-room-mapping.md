# SOP-WH-05 Temperature mapping of cold rooms

| Document ID | Version | Effective date | Owner | Approved by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SOP-WH-05 | 2.0 (draft) | – | Aleksandra Nowak | pending |

## 1. Purpose

Demonstrate that every cold room keeps the required temperature in all locations where product is stored.

## 2. Procedure

1. Place calibrated data loggers in a 3D grid (at least 9 loggers for rooms up to 50 m³, 15 above).
2. Record for **72 hours** with the room loaded as in normal operation.
3. **Perform the mapping twice: once in summer (July–August) and once in winter (January–February).**
4. Identify hot and cold spots. Product must not be stored in locations outside the range.
5. Place the permanent monitoring probe at the worst-case location found in either season.
6. Repeat after any change to the room or the cooling system, and at least every 3 years.

## 3. Records

Mapping report per room and season, stored with the hub's qualification documents.
