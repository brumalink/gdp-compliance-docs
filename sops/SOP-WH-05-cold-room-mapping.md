# SOP-WH-05 Temperature mapping of cold rooms

| Document ID | Version | Effective date | Owner | Approved by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SOP-WH-05 | 1.0 | 2025-09-02 | Aleksandra Nowak | Joanna Kowalczyk |

## 1. Purpose

Demonstrate that every cold room keeps the required temperature in all locations where product is stored.

## 2. Procedure

1. Place calibrated data loggers in a 3D grid (at least 9 loggers for rooms up to 50 m³, 15 above).
2. Record for **72 hours** with the room loaded as in normal operation.
3. Identify hot and cold spots. Product must not be stored in locations outside the range.
4. Place the permanent monitoring probe at the worst-case location.
5. Repeat after any change to the room or the cooling system, and at least every 3 years.

## 3. Records

Mapping report per room, stored with the hub's qualification documents.
